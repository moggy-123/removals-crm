1

Install on your Android phone

1

Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome.

2

Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).

3

Tap Install. Open Removals CRM from your home screen.

Then sign in with the email and password you were given.

Just open the app in your browser. In Chrome or Edge you can also click the install icon at the right of the address bar to keep it in your dock or taskbar.
